Mini Supermarket Management System

This document describes the complete implementation plan, database design, system architecture, GUI design, object-oriented design, and business logic for the Mini Supermarket Management System developed using Java, Swing GUI, JDBC, and MySQL.

1. Project Objective

The purpose of the Mini Supermarket Management System is to automate the management of products, categories, sales transactions, cashiers, stock control, reporting, and user authentication.

The system will:

  • Manage products and categories
  • Manage users and cashiers
  • Process customer sales
  • Maintain inventory levels
  • Generate reports
  • Store all records in a relational database

11. Business Rules Implementation

  1. Every product must have a unique product code.
  2. A customer can buy many products in one sale.
  3. Every sale is handled by one cashier.
  4. One cashier can process many sales.
  5. A sale must contain at least one product.
  6. Quantity sold cannot exceed stock quantity.
  7. Stock is reduced after successful sale.
  8. Each product belongs to one category.
  9. Each category can contain many products.
  10. Every sale records:
    • Date
    • Total Price
    • Payment Amount
